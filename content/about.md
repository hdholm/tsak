This site is where I keep notes about solutions I've found to various technical
problems, as well as solutions for various political and social ills.  I keep
them as a reference for myself, but hopefully others will find something useful
here.  Content changes over time as solutions improve, evolve, or are found to
be suboptimal.  So don't depend on something staying here forever.

Often the solution for political and social ills, while not perfect, are
blocked by parties that find the existing more broken systems benefit them at
the expense of the broader society.

This site is built with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) and the source is available
on [GitHub](https://github.com/hdholm/tsak/).  If you find something broken,
or have improvements to offer submit issues or pull requests there.
