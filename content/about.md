## Who am I

[I am by profession](https://hdholm.com/consultants/howard) a (mostly retired
now) cybersecurity professional. I created this site mostly for myself. I
sincerely hope that others can find some value in something here, but that's
really secondary to this being a place where I can leave notes to myself.
There are primarily three categories of things here:
- solutions to technical problems I've worked through
- thoughts on public policy that are often less full solutions than improvements
  on the current state of affairs, and
- random stuff that is usually culture or entertainment

You may not agree with things written here. If you believe you have an
improvement either substantive or grammatical that I've written about here feel
free to suggest the improvement as described below. I may not agree with
you---and you are free to publish your own words on your own site. That means
you shouldn't expect that all suggested updates will result in a change here.
If you suggest a change and I agree it is an improvement I will change it. Even
where you disagree with my position, I encourage you to try and change my mind.
If you have a well supported argument, I'll consider it.

***Content will change*** over time as solutions improve, evolve, or are found
to be suboptimal.  So don't depend on something staying here forever.

## Policy Article Notes

Often improvements for public policy ills are blocked by entities that find the
existing dysfunctional systems benefit them at the expense of the broader
society. Consequently, often what is most needed is not naming the
solution, but the political will to implement it.  I hope to some tiny extent
articles here can increase the political pressure for positive change. If you
desire policy solutions you see here, press your political representatives to
make it happen and pay attention when you vote to which candidates are actually
working to make positive solutions happen.
