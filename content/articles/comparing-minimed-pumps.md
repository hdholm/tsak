I have been using Minimed insulin pumps for many years.  I recently upgraded
fom a Minimed 780g to a Minimed Flex, and while overall the Flex is an upgrade,
there are some things I wish were differnt.  Both systems pair with a
continuous glucose monitor (CGM) and use that to enable automatic basal and
bolus insulin delivery without intervention.  The algorithm for the two pumps
is currently the same, although I belive some future software algorithm updates
may only be available for the Flex.

The Pros and Cons of the Flex with respect to the 780g
======================================================
- [+] The Flex is rechargable on it's own small magsafe stand and requires only
    about 30 minutes of charging a week as opposed to the AA batteries required
    by the 780g.
- [+] The Flex is controllable from a smart phone so it is less obtrusive. (It
    can be worn under clothes because you don't need to interact with it.)
- [-] The Flex can ONLY be controlled from a smart phone.  There is no way to
    see any real information on the pump itself (aside from LED color changes
    to indicate severity of alarms) and no way to control boluses or suspend
    the pump without the phone. This can be problematic in some environments.
- [+] The flex is somewhat smaller than the 780g although I find the 780g "small
    enough" for me.  In use the Flex does not feel that much smaller to me.
- [-] The Flex is new, so despite it being better suited to being worn under
    clothing, there are a dearth of good holster/belt solutions available. It
    ships with a belt clip which almost by definition is over clothing not
    under.

The Solutions are Known
=======================
To foot-stomp the theme of this blog, a solution of putting the technology of
the Flex (smart phone control, wireless charging, updated hardware) into the
form factor of the 780g while keeping the ability to control the pump from the
small screen and buttons on the pump itself would have allowed the continued
use of existing belts/holsters and the freedom of controling the pump without
a smart phone while maintaining essentially all the advantages of the Flex.
Nevertheless, that wasn't the chosen direction and while not perfect the Flex
so far has been a marginal improvement over the 780g.
