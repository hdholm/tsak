These five articles look at how American government and the economy are
organized, and at changes that would make them work better for the people they
are supposed to serve. Each stands on its own, but they are arranged in the
order I would read them: how we choose representatives, how money bends that
process, how the courts are chosen and held accountable, how economic power is
checked, and how healthcare could be organized. The theme of this site applies
to all of them: the solutions are known, and what is usually missing is the
will to use them.
